REPAIR SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

1. Acknowledgement
Every repair request is acknowledged within 24 hours of being logged. The customer
receives a repair ticket number at the time of acknowledgement.

2. Standard Repair SLA
Standard repairs are completed within 7 business days from the date the unit is
received at the service centre.

3. Complex Repairs
Repairs requiring part replacement, board-level work, or parts sourced from the
manufacturer may take up to 14 business days. The customer is informed as soon as a
repair is reclassified as complex.

4. Status Updates
The customer receives an SMS update at each stage of the repair: received,
diagnosis complete, repair in progress, quality check, and dispatched.

5. SLA Breach
If a repair exceeds the applicable SLA, the customer is entitled to a temporary
replacement unit at no cost until the repaired unit is returned.
