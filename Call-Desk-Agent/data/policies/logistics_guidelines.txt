LOGISTICS AND RETURNS GUIDELINES

1. Prepaid Return Label
All returns must use the prepaid return label sent to the customer by email.
Customers must not pay for return shipping; any self-paid courier charge is not
reimbursed unless a prepaid label could not be issued.

2. Packaging
Items must be packed in their original packaging wherever possible, including
accessories and manuals. Where the original box is unavailable, the customer is
advised to use rigid packaging with adequate padding.

3. Tracking
The return tracking number must be shared with the customer at the time of pickup
or drop-off, and confirmed on the call or in the follow-up email.

4. Refund Timeline
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of the item being received and
inspected at the warehouse. The refund is issued to the original payment method.

5. Lost In Transit
Claims for shipments lost in transit must be raised within 30 days of the dispatch
date. Claims raised after 30 days cannot be processed by the logistics partner.
