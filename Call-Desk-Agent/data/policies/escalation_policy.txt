ESCALATION POLICY

1. Escalation Triggers
A case is escalated when any of the following occurs: the issue remains unresolved
after 2 customer contacts, the customer explicitly asks to speak to a manager, or
an applicable SLA has been breached.

2. Assignment
An escalated case is assigned to a senior agent within 2 hours of the escalation
being raised. The senior agent owns the case until closure.

3. Customer Notification
The customer is notified of the escalation by both email and SMS, including the
escalation reference number and the name of the owning senior agent.

4. Escalation SLA
The escalation team must provide a resolution within 24 hours of the case being
assigned. If a resolution is not possible in 24 hours, the customer is called with
a status update and a committed date.

5. Records
Every escalation is logged with the trigger reason, owning agent, and resolution,
and is reviewed weekly by the quality team.
