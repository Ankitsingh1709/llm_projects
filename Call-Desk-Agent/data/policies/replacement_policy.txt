REPLACEMENT POLICY

1. Eligibility Window
A product is eligible for replacement only if the request is raised within 90 days
of the original date of purchase. Requests raised after 90 days are rejected and the
customer is offered a paid repair instead.

2. Proof of Purchase
The customer must provide valid proof of purchase (order ID, invoice number, or
receipt) before a replacement is approved. No replacement is processed without it.

3. Dispatch Timeline
Once a replacement is approved, the unit is dispatched within 3-5 business days.
The customer receives a dispatch confirmation with a tracking number.

4. One Replacement Per Order
Only one replacement is allowed per order. If the replacement unit also develops a
fault, the case is routed to the repair team under the repair SLA.

5. Exclusions
Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, liquid ingress, unauthorised repair, or
physical tampering is not eligible for replacement.
