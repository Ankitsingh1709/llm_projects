DEVICE SUPPORT POLICY

1. Support Hours
Device setup and configuration support is available Monday to Saturday,
9:00 am to 6:00 pm. Requests received outside these hours are answered on the
next working day.

2. Remote Support
Remote support over screen share is available for premium customers. Standard
customers are supported over phone, chat, or email with guided instructions.

3. First Contact Material
A basic troubleshooting guide is emailed to the customer on first contact, before
any advanced diagnostic step is attempted.

4. Escalation To Repair
Hardware issues that cannot be resolved through software troubleshooting are
escalated to the repair team, and the case then follows the repair SLA.

5. Scope
Device support covers setup, configuration, connectivity, firmware updates, and
basic diagnostics. It does not cover third-party software or physical damage.
